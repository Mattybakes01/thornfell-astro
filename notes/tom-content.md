## Full descriptions (hat pages)

### The Western Rose
Soft in texture, assured in silhouette. The Western Rose features a folded telescope crown, a gently lifted brim and a subtle drop over the eyes, shaped with quiet precision. Finished with a braided leather band and the Iron Thorn, each hat bears the restrained lustre of something made slowly and deliberately by hand.

An elegant, one-of-a-kind piece understated enough to wear anywhere, with the unmistakable character of a bespoke creation.

### The Heart of the West
Playful and feminine. The Heart of the West features a hand sculpted heart-shaped crown, a slight lifted brim with a soft pencil curl. Finished with a braided leather band and the Iron Thorn.

With the felt just lightly pounced to maintain a soft lustrous nap this hat is luxurious in look and feel.

### The Dresstern Gentleman
Refined without losing its edge. The Dresstern Gentleman features clean lines and a restrained brim width, creating a short-brimmed silhouette equally at home dressed up or worn out in the field. Finished with a genuine vintage python hatband, three .243 Winchester bullet casings and the Iron Thorn.

A versatile, one-of-a-kind piece that brings quiet sophistication to a distinctly rugged form.

### The Ivory Regent
Elegant and commanding. The Ivory Regent features a classically shaped cattleman crown, standing 5 inches tall with a soft, considered crease, balanced by a gently rolled 4¼-inch brim. A seamlessly joined felt self-band maintains its clean silhouette, finished with a single .243 Winchester casing.

Regal without excess—a refined statement piece defined by proportion, restraint and quiet authority.

### The Pallbearer
A dark twist on western form. The Pallbearer features a deeply sculpted skull set within a gothic coffin crown, creating a silhouette that reveals its most striking detail from above. Finished with a beautifully textured, vintage black python hatband, rustic barbed wire and the Iron Thorn.

Macabre, uncompromising and unmistakably Thornfell, a one-of-a-kind piece made for those unafraid to stand apart.

### The Bonecollector
The Bonecollector features a weathered, punisher style skull set within a short-brimmed gunslinger silhouette, marked by battle damage and roughly stitched scars. A genuine crocodile bone-leather band sits over hand-distressed petersham, bound at the base with rustic barbed wire and finished with the Iron Thorn.

Brutal, defiant and entirely unapologetic, this piece is the hat of a bounty hunter, carrying the scars of a life never lived quietly.

### The Outdoorsman
Purposeful and understated. The Outdoorsman features a wide, restrained pencil curled brim, with a flattened telescope crown. Dropped gently at the front and rear, the brim is shaped to shed rain and offer protection from the sun without compromising its clean silhouette. Finished with a 20mm leather hatband and a single .243 Winchester cartridge.

Practical, dependable and quietly distinctive. A hat made for open ground, changing weather and a life lived outdoors.
